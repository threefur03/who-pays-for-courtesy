Exploratory microscopic and macroscopic analysis (v6, not registered)

Written on 9 November 2026 while the grid was running (522 of 960 episodes done) and before any grid outcome was read. The script was tested only on the gate runs in runs/gates/G6.

The registered analysis is unchanged: analysis/aggregate_v6.py, H5–H8 in DESIGN_H.md section 5, frozen in integrity/REGISTRATION_FREEZE_H.json.

Everything here is labelled exploratory in the paper. It is descriptive and no prediction is attached to it.

Why

The paper is framed as a benchmark of current driving and signal paradigms. It reports how cars and people share the zebra at two levels:

  • Microscopic: single encounters and single people. Who entered the conflict band first, how close the two passed, and which kinds of crosser bear the cost.
  • Macroscopic: scenario totals. The registered who-pays measures (person·s held or slowed, vehicle·s standing), plus who gets through within the green.

The registered contrasts already cover the scenario-level totals. This file adds the breakdowns that explain them.

Definitions (fixed before the grid finished)

Inputs. The script reads only the per-run files the batch runner writes: CONFIG.json, MEASURES.json, PROGRESS.json and COMPLETION.json. It uses the measure code's own encounter list:

  • an encounter is a person–vehicle pair whose occupancy of the shared stretch of zebra comes within 5 s;
  • PET is the second entry minus the first exit;
  • a close pass is PET < 1.5 s.

Microscopic measures

ID Measure Breakdown
M1 Share of car encounters (car_0–car_2) in which the person entered first by time since the car's green began (< 10, 10–20, ≥ 20 s, the SinD reference bins, and 5 s bins for the figure), by signal program and lead paradigm
M2 Close-pass share and PET quartiles by vehicle role (lead car, follower cars, motorcycle) and signal program; lead-car close passes by paradigm
M3 Per-person held + slowed by vehicles (s), for crossers who entered the zebra by crosser kind (walker, runner, e-bike), lead paradigm and signal program

Macroscopic measures

ID Measure Breakdown
A1 Who gets through: cars completing the full turn within the green (of 3), and the share of crossers who cleared the zebra within the green by lead paradigm and signal program
A2 Scenarios that discriminate the paradigms: (i) the lead car met a crosser in at least one condition; (ii) the three paradigms' person held + slowed, each averaged over follower law and signal, span more than 1 person·s by scenario source (S0 calibrated, S1 LLM)
A3 Spearman correlation of scenario person held + slowed, and of car standing, with the green G and the flashing time F (the exploratory G/F relation named in DESIGN_H.md section 5) by signal program

Intervals. 95% percentile bootstrap that resamples scenarios (2000 resamples, seed 9). An encounter or person-level share is pooled within the resampled scenarios. These intervals are descriptive.

Real-log reference. The paper compares M1 and the PET distribution with the SinD left-turn encounters (experiments/real_log_context). The SinD junction runs the concurrent program, so only the concurrent arm is set beside it. Geometry and queue timing differ, so the comparison is a reference, not a validation.
